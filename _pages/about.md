I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability. I am advised by Professor Junxian He.
